# Space Minesweeper

Try the feeling of detecting mines in space.

## About The Game

Henry is Earth's spaceship pilot, tasked with exploring mysterious regions of the galaxy.

Unfortunately, he got lost in a dangerous area filled with hidden bombs. No one knows what force created them, but now Henry needs your help to survive.

Use your logic and careful judgment to detect where the mines are hidden. Mark those dangerous positions with flags so Henry can avoid them and continue the mission safely.

In addition, the game includes multiple music tracks, making it a simple and relaxing experience to enjoy anytime.

## Gameplay

- Explore a grid full of hidden dangers
- Use logic to identify where mines are located
- Place flags to mark dangerous positions
- Clear the board without triggering the bombs

## Why Play Space Minesweeper?

- Space-themed minesweeper experience
- Easy to understand and simple to play
- Great for relaxing while still using your brain
- Includes music to make the experience more enjoyable
